Introduction & Architecture
Free to Read

This guide documents how to build a fully automated Instagram content agent that generates posts using AI, uploads images, and publishes to Instagram and Facebook — completely hands-free, every single day. No coding background required. Every error you might hit is documented with an exact fix.

What the Agent Does

Every day at your scheduled time, the agent wakes up on GitHub's servers and completes 5 steps automatically — without you touching anything. Here's exactly what happens:

1
Token Check
Verifies your Meta access token is valid. Auto-refreshes if expiring within 5 days — you never lose access.
2
Content Generation via Claude AI
Calls the Claude API to write a complete post — hook, caption, carousel slides, 30 hashtags, and an image prompt. Language alternates between Bahasa Indonesia and English by day.
3
Image Pipeline
Fetches a cover image from Unsplash (free), then uploads it to Cloudinary to generate a public URL that Meta's API can access.
4
Instagram Publishing
Publishes to Instagram via the official Meta Graph API — single image or carousel depending on the day of the week.
5
Facebook Cross-Post
Optionally cross-posts the same content to your Facebook Page simultaneously — reaching both audiences with one action.

The Technology Stack

Every service used in this guide is either free or costs less than a cup of coffee per month. Here's what runs under the hood:

ℹ️

Claude API — generates captions, slides, and hashtags. Cost: approximately $1–5/month at 1 post per day. A $5 top-up lasts several months.

✅

Unsplash + Cloudinary + GitHub Actions + GitHub Pages — all completely free. Zero hosting, zero server, zero monthly fees.

⚠️

Meta Graph API — free but has a 25 posts/day limit. At 1 post/day you will never hit this. During setup testing you might — it resets at 07:00 WIB each morning.

Content Strategy — 12 Pillars, 2 Languages

The agent doesn't just post randomly. It follows a structured content strategy with 12 rotating pillars — 5 evergreen topics and 7 crisis pillars specifically designed around the June 2026 Iran-US war economic impact on Indonesia.

The 7 crisis pillars include: Crisis Survival, Protect Your Money, Investing During Crisis, Rupiah Weakening, Beat Inflation, Winner's Mindset, and the Oil Shock Explained. All are grounded in real figures — Rp18,000/USD, 4.76% inflation, Rp500T budget pressure — making the content feel authoritative and timely rather than generic.
